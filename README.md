## About

This work presents a structural account of learning in systems that
persist under irreversible time. Learning is formalized as commitment
revision constrained by preservation of existing binding structure.
Updates that preserve commitments maintain identity and coherence, while
updates that violate binding lead to fragmentation and instability.

Catastrophic forgetting is reinterpreted as commitment violation rather
than information loss, and continual learning is shown to succeed only
when updates remain compatible with accumulated commitments. The paper
identifies architectural requirements for identity-preserving learning
without assuming reversible modification or unrestricted overwrite.
